PART III

### WHAT STABILIZED CONFIGURATIONS DO TO OTHER PEOPLE

This part examines the interpersonal consequences of systems that have stabilized by exporting regulation rather than containing it internally.
Once stabilization has occurred, interaction becomes consequential. Regulation completes through proximity, access, and asymmetry, altering the coherence of adjacent systems. The effects described here do not arise from intention or conflict, but from structural routing within constrained relationships.
