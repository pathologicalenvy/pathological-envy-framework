PART IV

# HOW FAILURE SPREADS

This part traces what occurs once stabilized systems complete regulation outside themselves and the cost of that regulation exceeds the capacity of adjacent systems.
Failure is not contained where it originates. It propagates along existing pathways of access and asymmetry, concentrating in predictable locations. What appears as isolated breakdown, personal collapse, or institutional dysfunction is shown here as downstream residue of displaced regulation moving through a shared ecology.
