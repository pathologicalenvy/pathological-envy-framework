Figure A6. Prediction bandwidth under stabilization.
As stabilization increases, the space of viable responses narrows, reducing adaptive bandwidth while increasing patterned recurrence. Predictability emerges not from determinism or intent, but from constraint: behavior varies within increasingly limited bounds as rigidity rises. This conditional legibility marks the completion of the closed-system account and establishes the structural basis for downstream consequences once interaction with other systems occurs.
