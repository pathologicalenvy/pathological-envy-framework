Figure A4. Borderline organization as junction.
Borderline organization is represented as a junctional configuration arising from incomplete or unstable reorganization following collapse. Rather than constituting a fixed endpoint, it functions as a transient routing condition from which multiple stabilization pathways remain accessible. Instability here reflects unresolved constraint negotiation rather than severity or failure.
